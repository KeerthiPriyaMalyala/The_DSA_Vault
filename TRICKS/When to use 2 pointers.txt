🧠 When should I think of i & j pointers?

First ask yourself this question:

"Can I solve this by scanning the array only once?"

If yes 👉 Two pointers is probably the answer.

Think:

"I want to move/copy/replace elements."

🎯 Super Trick (This is what I use)

Whenever I see an array question, I ask these 5 questions:

1️⃣ Is the array sorted?
➡️ Think left & right pointers.

2️⃣ Am I removing, shifting, or overwriting elements?
➡️ Think i = write, j = read.

3️⃣ Am I comparing the first and last elements?
➡️ Think left & right.

4️⃣ Am I looking for a continuous subarray/substring?
➡️ Think sliding window (i & j).

5️⃣ Am I trying to split or partition the array?
➡️ Think boundary pointer + scan pointer.
